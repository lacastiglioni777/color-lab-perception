03 / THE IDEA

There's more to color
than meets the eye.

Inspired by the color-perception experiments of artist and educator Josef Albers. Students make predictions, observe evidence, and create their own tests.

01 / LOOK

Notice the context.

The same swatch can seem lighter, darker, warmer, or cooler depending on its neighbors.

02 / TEST

Get evidence.

The backgrounds can push identical colors apart, or make different colors seem closer. The reveal brings the swatches together.

03 / MAKE

Become the artist.

Change the colors in the studio and discover how much control you have over what someone else sees.

What surrounds a color can change what we think we see. That is the experiment.
